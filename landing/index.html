About AION

AI works inside your company.
Your data stays there.

AION is the on-premise AI suite developed by ASA Computer. Models and agents run on hardware the company owns, at its own site, working with the documents and systems people use every day.

We build AI systems that fit your processes, not the other way around: from service desk to server analysis, from asset management to internal knowledge.
